Use this prompt in your design tool or with a developer:

***

Redesign the website footer for the “Emotion AI Platform – SceneApps” to improve **readability**, visibility, and overall aesthetics.

Current footer elements to keep:
- SceneApps logo and name  
- Tagline about redefining human connection through emotion AI  
- “LEGAL” section with Privacy Policy and Terms of Service  
- “CONTACT” section with the support email  
- Copyright line for 2026 SceneApps Incorporated  

Design goals:
1. Readability  
- Set base footer text size to 14–16px with comfortable line-height.[1]
- Create a clear hierarchy: brand name/logo as the primary element, then tagline, then legal/contact text in smaller type.[1]
- Avoid awkward line breaks so sentences like the copyright appear on a single line when possible.[1]

2. Visibility and contrast  
- Choose a footer background that contrasts well with the main page while maintaining brand colors (e.g., slightly darker or lighter band).[1]
- Ensure strong contrast between text and background; adjust colors to meet accessibility guidelines.[1]
- Style links (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, email) with a distinct color and clear hover/focus states.[1]

3. Layout and structure  
- Use a three-column layout on desktop:  
  - Left: logo + short tagline  
  - Middle: “Legal” links  
  - Right: “Contact” with email  
- Align elements neatly and add consistent vertical and horizontal spacing.[1]
- Add sufficient padding above and below the footer to visually separate it from the preceding content.[1]

4. Branding and aesthetics  
- Match the typography (font family, weights, letter spacing) to the rest of the site.[1]
- Make the logo area (icon + “SceneApps”) clean and clickable to the homepage.[1]
- Optionally add subtle accents: a thin divider line above the footer, small icon next to the email, and minimal social icons if appropriate to the brand.[1]

Deliverables:
- Updated footer layout for desktop and mobile (responsive)  
- Color, typography, and spacing specs  
- Implementation-ready HTML/CSS suggestions or a component spec that a developer can build from.[1]
