3. All Core Features (Highlight Everything)

👁️ 1. Real-Time Emotion Analysis
	•	The AI detects facial expressions, micro-reactions, and vocal tones in real time.
	•	It identifies emotions like joy, surprise, frustration, calm, and empathy.
	•	Results appear as transparent emotion scores for creators, users, and brands to understand genuine audience response.

🗣️ 2. Intelligent Verbal Mentions (Natural Spoken Mentions)
	•	This is a breakthrough feature: users don’t need to use “@” symbols or text tags.
	•	When someone naturally says another person’s name aloud in their video — for example,
“Daniel, you really need to see this,” or “I think Nike did something brilliant here,” —
the AI automatically recognizes the spoken name, identifies who they’re referring to, and triggers a video mention notification to that person.
	•	The mentioned user can only respond through a video reply, keeping interactions authentic and emotionally rich.
	•	It mirrors real-life communication — conversational, expressive, and emotionally layered — while powered by real-time speech recognition and AI understanding.
	•	This feature transforms mentions from text-based tags into human dialogue.

❤️ 3. Authentic Reaction Capture
	•	Users can only post real-time video reactions, not pre-recorded or edited videos.
	•	This ensures every reaction captured is spontaneous and emotionally genuine.
	•	The platform prioritizes truth over performance.

🧠 4. AI Reaction Grading System
	•	Every video and reaction is analyzed across five emotional and behavioral metrics:
	•	Authenticity
	•	Emotional Intensity
	•	Tone & Mood Balance
	•	Engagement Depth
	•	Sentiment Polarity (positive/neutral/negative)
	•	Grading is transparent and visible, allowing creators and brands to understand the real emotional impact of their content.

📊 5. Emotion Insights Dashboard
	•	Each creator or brand has a dashboard summarizing audience emotion over time.
	•	Displays metrics like happiness ratio, empathy rate, excitement peaks, and average sentiment trends.
	•	Turns emotional data into visual storytelling.

🧩 6. Brand & Business Emotion Analytics
	•	Businesses can access aggregated emotional insights about how people react to their videos or products.
	•	Data can be filtered by:
	•	Keywords or verbal mentions (e.g., “Nike”)
	•	Emotional category (e.g., joy, curiosity, trust)
	•	Demographics or campaign type.
	•	They see authentic audience emotion, not manipulated engagement numbers.
	•	Only public videos and mentions are analyzed — private ones are never touched.

🛡️ 7. Privacy and Authenticity Safeguards
	•	Privacy is foundational.
	•	Only publicly shared videos and spoken mentions are processed.
	•	Private messages, DMs, or non-consensual data are off-limits.
	•	AI works silently in the background — it observes, never interferes.

🏅 8. Reward & Incentive System
	•	Users earn points, badges, or tokens for consistent authentic engagement.
	•	The more real and emotionally rich your interactions, the higher your recognition.
	•	Rewards prioritize sincerity over virality.

🧬 9. The AI Social Brain
	•	The AI functions as the silent brain of the platform.
	•	It learns emotional context and interaction patterns — not to manipulate, but to understand.
	•	It helps surface authentic, emotionally resonant content without addictive algorithms.

👥 10. For All User Types
	•	Creators: Share real moments, understand audience emotions, and grow through authenticity.
	•	Viewers: React naturally, express themselves, and connect emotionally.
	•	Businesses: Access real-time emotion data to understand how people truly feel.
