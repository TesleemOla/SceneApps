SceneApps is a more natural way to connect over video content with friends, family, and people you follow or follows you. It let's you capture and selectively share your authentic reactions to video content privately or publicly and keeps you notified of every verbal mention of you in videos posted by people you follow or follows you, keeping you up to date on every conversation where your name is mentioned.
