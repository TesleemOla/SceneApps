This Privacy Policy outlines how SceneApps collects, uses, and safeguards your data when you use our platform.

Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create a SceneApps account, we collect your name, date of birth, channel name and phone number.

Profile Information: You have the option to provide additional information such as your profile video, bio, and other details to personalize your profile.

Video Content: We collect and store the videos you upload, as well as the reactions and interactions with your videos.

Usage Data: We collect information about how you use SceneApps, such as your browsing activity, device information, and IP address.

How We Use Your Information

Personalization: We may use your information to personalize your SceneApps experience, recommend content, and connect you with other users.

Profile Communication: We may send you updates, notifications, and promotional messages related to SceneApps.

Analytics: We analyze user data to improve our services, troubleshoot issues, and enhance security.

Legal Compliance: We may use your information to comply with legal obligations or respond to legal requests.

Sharing Your Information

Public Sharing: Videos and profile information you choose to make public will be visible to other SceneApps users.

Third Parties: We may share your data with trusted third-party service providers for purposes like data storage and analytics.

Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information in response to legal requests, court orders, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

Your Choices

Account Settings: You can manage your privacy settings, control who can see your content, and customize notifications in your account settings.

Data Access: You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. Contact us at contact@sceneapps.com

Opt-Out: You can opt-out of receiving promotional emails from us by following the instructions in the email.

Security Measures

Data Security: We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access and misuse.

Account Security: Keep your account credentials confidential and notify us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.

Children's Privacy

SceneApps is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any significant changes.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about your privacy or this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@sceneapps.com
